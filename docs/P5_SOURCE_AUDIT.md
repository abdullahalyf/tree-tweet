## P5 claim-to-action map

The identifiers below are an **office-visit and paper-comparison recommendation**. Follow the current official service's actual instructions. Users can read the guide with missing papers; do not demand private uploads to begin.

### 1. Owner and plot identity

**Current claim:** compare seller identity, deed and khatian; use a full location/survey context; ask the sub-registry about copies and the transfer chain. A khatian alone is not a title guarantee. The card describes learning what to compare rather than checking a connected record.

**Expected information:** division/district/upazila or thana, mouza name and JL where shown, survey/record type, old/current khatian and dag if different, area/share, deed number/date/year and registering office. Compare original identities privately; where the seller's name or role differs, ask the appropriate office and qualified adviser about authority to sell. These are separate checks, not a fuzzy-name match.

**Source map:** S2 supports record-field context; S3 supports deed preparation; S4/S5 identify the official copy route; S12 supports sub-registry copies. [Open DLRMS](https://dlrms.land.gov.bd/).

**Fallback and unknown:** records/copies may need the relevant district record room, land office or sub-registry. An unavailable copy, duplicate-looking name, unlinked transfer or mismatched area stays unresolved. A matching name/number or a digitally unchanged document does not establish a truthful original or current selling authority.

**Assessment:** current guide/help already cover the central safeguard. Add JL and deed date to the compact paper list for consistency with the glossary; this is a useful improvement rather than a broken flow.

### 2. Mutation papers and history

**Current claim:** obtain mutation khatian/order, reference and earlier papers, compare plots/area, and ask AC (Land) about gaps. The full history may be absent online. Supporting FAQ explicitly separates an application from a decision.

**Expected information:** mutation application/case reference with year if present, decision/order date, resulting khatian and previous record references, plot context and area. Sensitive tracking fields, if required, belong only on the official service. Do not infer approval from an application screenshot, SMS or fee receipt alone.

**Source map:** S2/S3 support the record-update purpose and stages. [Open mutation service](https://mutation.land.gov.bd/). A current application's tracking screen is not established here as a nationwide chronological ownership history.

**Fallback and unknown:** AC (Land)/relevant land office, using the original file and earlier/current documents. Missing earlier stages remain an incomplete history even if the latest paper exists. “Papers collected” only records user preparation.

**Assessment:** wording is appropriately cautious. Do not add a “history verified” badge or scrape private tracking identifiers as part of this redesign.

### 3. Cases and court orders

**Current claim:** ask for a case reference and court, consult the cause list and revenue service separately, and confirm orders/plot identity with the relevant court/office and a lawyer. An empty search does not prove no case.

**Expected information:** **case number and four-digit year**, court name/type, division/district, relevant hearing/order date and the land schedule or documents connecting that case to this plot. A party-name match is insufficient. S7 expressly supports the omitted year; the remaining comparison is practical due diligence rather than a portal requirement invented by this app.

**Official routes:** [Judiciary cause list](https://causelist.judiciary.gov.bd/), [case-search entry](https://causelist.judiciary.gov.bd/caseinfo), [revenue-case service](https://case.gov.bd/). S6–S8 support the distinct published purposes. Revenue service also describes civil-case response functions, so do not overstate a clean separation of every record type.

**Fallback and unknown:** relevant court for the current order and file, relevant land office for revenue proceedings, and a land-matter lawyer for interpretation. Pending/disposed status, an old hearing list or no result must not be translated into permission to sell or absence of an injunction.

**Assessment:** retain current warnings; add case year in the main step. This is the strongest specific copy change established by fresh evidence.

### 4. Government khas status

**Current claim:** ask about the latest record/classification and compare relevant khas register/records at Union Land Office and AC (Land). The app expressly lacks direct access to that register. The government gateway link is accurately named a service entry point, not a khas-clearance search.

**Expected information:** full plot context, khatian and area/share, latest record/copy date, recorded ownership and any government-interest/settlement papers shown. Record possession or a seller's story separately from recorded status. Local rules and documents require office interpretation.

**Source map:** S9 supports the office-record investigation route, with its age and settlement-specific scope. S1 provides the [land-service entry point](https://www.land.gov.bd/). No fresh source established a public nationwide khas-status query or any third-party API.

**Fallback and unknown:** Union/municipal land office where applicable and AC (Land), asking how to obtain written information or the relevant official copy. A page failure, missing online record or absence of a warning cannot become “not khas.” Nor should “government interest mentioned” trigger an automatic legal verdict on every transfer.

**Assessment:** main wording is safe; add an explicit missing-record sentence directly in this step for users who never open the FAQ.

### 5. Acquisition notices and cases

**Current claim:** ask about project notices/LA case/land schedule, match mouza and plot, and confirm with the Deputy Commissioner's LA branch. The page says LARMS is not an open search covering every plot. The card is an information path, not a status result.

**Expected information:** district, upazila, mouza/JL, survey, khatian/dag and affected area; LA case number/year if known; project name; original notice reference/date, issuing office and attached schedule/map. Do not infer whether a specific statutory stage, compensation right or restriction applies from a generic notice title.

**Source map:** S5/S11 support the district LA office and land-schedule context. S10 supports the [LARMS entry point](https://larms.land.gov.bd/) with uncertain notice completeness and future-date warning. No particular notice was relied on for an individual's land.

**Fallback and unknown:** district administration's LA branch; ask which file/notice/schedule applies. No result, absent notice download or a public project map without a matching schedule stays unresolved. A partial-area acquisition must not be projected over the entire plot from a numeric match alone.

**Assessment:** current limit is good. Add an explicit “no online notice is not clearance” sentence in the step. Do not publish the future-dated item, marketing automation claims or an all-clear certificate.

## Missing-evidence behavior for any future P5 integration

These are product acceptance recommendations, not current implemented API behavior:

1. Keep **not checked**, **information unavailable**, **conflicting information** and **specific record found** distinct. Never map a timeout, 403, authentication requirement, empty result or missing document to a clean status.
2. A record found requires provenance: official source URL, relevant office/jurisdiction, complete identifiers, record/notice/order reference and date, and the time it was obtained. A homepage check date must never substitute for a plot-record date.
3. Do not merge two plots by dag alone, two people by similar names, two cases without court/year, or acquisition area without the matching land schedule.
4. A user marking collected is personal page state. It is neither proof the paper was seen by TreeTweet nor proof its content is authentic/current.
5. Keep legal interpretation separate from matching a record. Case disposition, mutation approval, tax payment, a QR response or an unmodified hash does not by itself establish unrestricted sale rights.
6. Public government hosting does not grant a supported API, republishing licence or authority to gather personal records. Authorized integration needs its own access/terms/privacy/provenance review. Nothing in this audit supplies that permission.
