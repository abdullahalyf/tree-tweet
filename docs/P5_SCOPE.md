# P5 — জমি কেনার আগে যাচাই / Check Before You Buy

User steering on 2026-10-18: mobile first for rural citizens, Bangla default, straightforward words. P5 is the main product; registration and tamper demonstrations are supporting tools.

## Five buyer questions
1. Does the seller match the recorded owner?
2. What does the mutation status and dated history show?
3. Is a court dispute reported?
4. Is the land recorded as government khas land?
5. Is acquisition or an acquisition notice reported?

Missing information must be visible and must not be treated as a clear result. Known risks remain visible alongside missing records. Risk assessments do not certify title or connect to government services.

## Citizen presentation
Primary results explain the problem, its meaning and the next action in plain Bangla or English. Hashes, algorithm names, diagnostic codes and technical engine text belong in a closed optional details section. The P5 checklist stays prominent. Demo tools are secondary in navigation.
